    How the tier is decided

    Three rules are applied in order. First, any single Central Licence trigger decides it: turnover above twenty crore rupees, importing, exporting, a hundred per cent export oriented unit, operating as an e-commerce food business operator, operating in two or more states, premises inside an airport or seaport, or a production capacity above what a State Licence covers. Second, if none of those apply, a petty food business operator — turnover, food capacity and milk handling all at or below the petty ceilings — needs only a Basic Registration. Third, anything in between needs a State Licence.

    Every threshold that fires, and every one that clears, is listed back to you, because knowing why you are in a tier is what stops you applying for the wrong one.

    The regulation

    The three tiers come from the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 and the Food Safety and Standards (Licensing and Registration of Food Businesses) Regulations, 2011. Applications are filed on FoSCoS, the Food Safety Compliance System run by FSSAI. A Basic Registration is applied for in Form A; a State or Central Licence in Form B.

    Where your data lives

    Everything you type stays on this machine. The licences you save are written to one local JSON file, shown under the watchdog table above. Nothing is sent anywhere, there is no account, and the tool makes no outbound network calls.

    Indicative figures used, and how to change them

    These are the thresholds, ceilings and fees the answer above is built from. They are indicative: FSSAI revises them from time to time. If a figure has changed, correct it here and the decision will follow your figure instead. Confirm the current figures on the FoSCoS portal before you apply.

    This tool is a planning aid, not a filing service and not legal advice. It does not submit anything to FSSAI on your behalf and it cannot see your state's own additions to the requirements. Fees, turnover thresholds and capacity ceilings change; the document list varies by state, by food category and by the kind of entity you are. Confirm your eligibility, the current fee and the document list on the official FoSCoS portal, or with your state's Food Safety Commissionerate, before you apply or renew.
